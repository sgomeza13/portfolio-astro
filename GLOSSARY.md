# Portfolio

Simon's personal site: it presents his work as a backend- and infrastructure-focused software engineer, mainly to hiring managers and secondarily to other engineers.

## Content

**Post**:
A single piece of published content on the site. Every Post has exactly one Kind and exists in one or more Locales.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, item

**Kind**:
The category of a Post: Project, Case Study, Role or Note. Every Kind is the same kind of thing, a blog-style Post; Kind only says what it is about.
_Avoid_: Type, category, tag

**Project**:
A Post about something Simon built, shown briefly: what it is, its stack, and optional links, images or video.
_Avoid_: Showcase, work item

**Case Study**:
A long-form Post that walks through problem, constraints, architecture, decisions and outcome of a piece of work.
_Avoid_: Deep dive, write-up

**Role**:
A Post about a position Simon held (e.g. Tech Lead/SWE at RevAIsor), limited to responsibilities, leadership and impact; it never discloses architecture or screenshots of confidential systems.
_Avoid_: Experience, job, position

**Note**:
A short, opinionated technical Post not tied to a specific Project or Role (e.g. a lesson learned, a technique).
_Avoid_: Article, TIL, snippet

**Tag**:
A technology or topic label attached to a Post (e.g. "PostgreSQL", "GCP").
_Avoid_: Skill, label, chip

## Localization

**Locale**:
A language the site is published in: English (`en`) or Spanish (`es`). A Post's text exists once per Locale.
_Avoid_: Language, lang, translation
