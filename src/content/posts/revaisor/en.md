RevAIsor builds a platform for auditing AI systems against regulatory frameworks such as the EU AI Act. I joined as a full-stack developer in January 2024 and have led the engineering team for over a year, while still shipping code across the backend, the infrastructure and the interfaces.

## Leading the team

I run a cross-functional team, turning stakeholder requirements into Spring Boot and Next.js deliverables. Since I took the lead, feature delivery times dropped by 30% and deployments became noticeably more stable across three core product modules.

## Performance

- I traced which critical endpoints were slowing the platform down and why, then designed a refactor that cut their wait times by at least 60%.
- I designed and tuned the MySQL databases behind the core modules for high availability, cutting query latency by 50% over a twelve-month improvement cycle.
- I took part in redesigning our CI/CD pipelines: builds went from 30 minutes to 7, and passing tests became a hard requirement to merge.

## Security

- I designed the multi-tenant security model in the core Spring Boot service, and secured internal service-to-service calls with signed tokens.
- I designed, built and deployed a serverless authorization service in Go and htmx that controls access to private document invitations in production.

## Product

I shipped explainability features end to end, from Spring Boot services to Next.js interfaces, and laid out a modular structure for the risk management, audit workflow and AI testing components so that both technical and non-technical users could work with them.
